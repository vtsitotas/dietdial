Delete your DietDial account

In the app

  1. Open DietDial and go to the Goals tab.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google.

Without the app

Email billtsitisback@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my DietDial account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account and everything linked to it on our servers: your email address, username and display name, daily nutrition totals, friendships and friend requests, badges, and coach relationships. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data stored only on your phone (your food log, recipes, weight entries and settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Anonymous usage statistics are not linked to your account and are not affected.

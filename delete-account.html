Delete your DietDial account

In the app

  1. Open DietDial and go to the Goals tab.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google.

Without the app

Email billtsitisback@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my DietDial account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account and everything linked to it on our servers: your email address, username, daily nutrition totals, friendships and friend requests, badges, and coach relationships. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data stored only on your phone (your food log, recipes, weight entries and settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Anonymous diet statistics are not linked to your account, so they are not affected; they are deleted automatically after 13 months. If you subscribed to DietDial Pro, Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

  • Data on your phone (food log, recipes, weight entries, goals, adaptive plan and check-ins): delete entries in the app, or remove all of it with Android Settings → Apps → DietDial → Storage → Clear data.
  • Friends and coaches: remove a friend or a coach in the Social tab. They immediately lose access to your data.
  • Anonymous diet statistics: turn off Goals → Share anonymous diet statistics. Collection stops and the random ID is deleted from your phone.
  • Data on our servers (your username, daily nutrition totals, badges): email billtsitisback@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my DietDial data”, and tell us what to delete (for example “all my daily totals”). We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

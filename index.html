Track what you eat, reach your goals.

DietDial is a free nutrition tracker for Android.

  • Log meals from a database of over 100,000 foods, or scan a barcode
  • Calorie ring, protein / carbs / fat and 30+ micronutrients
  • Water tracker, intermittent fasting timer and weight log
  • Recipes, favourites and custom foods
  • Optional: compare streaks with friends, or share your progress with a coach

Your food log stays on your phone. Only if you choose a username for the social features are daily totals stored on our servers — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what, and who can see it.
